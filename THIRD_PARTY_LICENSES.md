## NuFast

`nuosclab/nufast.py` is a pure-Python port of the long-baseline oscillation
algorithm from `NuFast_LBL.cpp`, by Peter B. Denton and Stephen J. Parke
([NuFast: Fast and accurate neutrino oscillation probabilities in matter](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.02400),
arXiv:2405.02400), <https://github.com/PeterDenton/NuFast-LBL>.
